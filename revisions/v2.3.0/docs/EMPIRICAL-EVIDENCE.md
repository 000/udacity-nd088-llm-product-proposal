# Assumptions, synthetic data and future validation

Revision 2.3.0. ND088 asks for a plan using a hypothetical scenario, not a deployed product or real company dataset. The candidate uses supplied Scenario C facts, explicit planning assumptions and a reproducible synthetic illustration. These sources are sufficient to explain the proposal's reasoning without claiming observed customer performance.

## Evidence used in the proposal

| Evidence type | Use and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Scenario givens | Preserve the supplied users, languages, devices, articles, topics, daily queries, parts and models. They are assignment context. |
| Planning assumptions | Label adoption, eligibility, time savings, labor rates, budgets and KPI thresholds. The illustrative $1,634 monthly net capacity value is arithmetic under those assumptions, not measured savings. |
| Supplied simulation | The inspected generator reproduces its canonical CSV hash. It generates 1,500 sessions with a 240-item timed sample; SYNTHETIC-BASELINE-AUDIT.md records the results, assumptions and limits. No proprietary logs or real PII underlie those rows. |
| Public documentation | Support specific model/API, retrieval, privacy, competitor and safety-context claims with the recorded source limits. Documentation does not demonstrate LineGuide performance. |

The simulation's language effects and outcomes are generated by its assumptions. They cannot establish real causal effects, workforce wage mix or customer value. Keep customer search labor separate from OEM support labor; combined capacity is not one firm's cash savings. No customer interview, live benchmark, production approval or safety certification is claimed.

## Proposed future implementation validation

If the fictional company implements the proposal, the plan can begin with a two-week discovery stage. The proposed $11,000 allowance, within the conditional $65,000 plan, is a product-planning choice rather than an academic completion condition.

A research lead could observe 25 authorized technicians completing eight distinct current-search tasks each, yielding a proposed 200 baseline observations. Domain reviewers would verify resolution, escalation and applicable source revisions. Engineering would record current quotations and finance would distinguish redeployable time from cash effects. A sponsor would compare the evidence with a stated investment rule before choosing stop, improve search, revise or continue the proposed rollout.

Later assistant comparisons would use separate held-out tasks, distinct matched variants and counterbalanced order. The five proposed product measurements provide the outcomes and guardrails. Domain/safety, access, failure and load tests are future implementation work; they are not prerequisites to publishing this hypothetical proposal.

[baseline-template.csv](baseline-template.csv) is an empty optional real-data collection schema, separate from the supplied simulation. Its fields cover task codes, comparison groups, eligibility, duration, independently verified resolution, escalation, source revisions and support time. Missing follow-up is not success. Any future records would stay in an approved protected store; publish only authorized aggregates, with no technician identities, serial numbers, proprietary instructions or raw queries.
