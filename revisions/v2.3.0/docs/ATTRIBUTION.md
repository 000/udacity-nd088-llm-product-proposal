# Attribution and provenance

LineGuide revision 2.3.0, 16 November 2026.

AI agents contributed substantive product reasoning, architecture choices, calculations, prose, diagrams, slide content, build scripts and verification work. This revision continues that workflow. The record does not establish independent student authorship or human verification of every judgment. Earlier v2 assertions of human-origin reasoning and universal human verification remain unsupported and are superseded by this statement.

The user reported no course-specific permission for substantive AI production. This package invents no permission, personal-authorship declaration, grade result or classroom confirmation. No classroom submission or own-work attestation is recorded. The historical [policy-review record](../../../docs/academic-integrity.md) retains its original scope; the [rubric map](RUBRIC-MAP.md) separately identifies the actual proposal-content requirements.

Scenario facts are hypothetical assignment givens. Budgets, configuration choices and targets are labelled proposals. The supplied simulation is explicitly synthetic and reproducible; it is not customer telemetry, an interview, measured savings or an LLM benchmark. The empty real-data template remains separate. Public sources carry claim/access limits, including metadata/preview-only access to the assignment-linked HBR article. No completed human review, production approval or industrial safety certification is asserted.
