# Reference data and maintenance

All quantities below describe the supplied scenario or proposed pilot scope. No company documents, catalog data or customer tasks were obtained or indexed for this proposal.

| Material | Inventory and proposed pilot | Source and approval | Quality/bias checks | Refresh proposal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Controlled reference documents | Scenario: 7,500 English articles and 25 topics across 15 models. Pilot: up to 200 approved articles, five topics and two models | Company document owners provide licensed, approved originals with revision, applicability, warnings and access metadata | Audit source gaps by model/topic; check diagrams/OCR and warning completeness. Missing or contradictory applicability blocks indexing | Process approved change events; daily reconciliation is a backstop. Withdrawal blocks display immediately through the approval register |
| Parts records | Scenario: 45,000 parts. Pilot: the compatible records for the two selected models; actual eligible count is unknown | Authoritative company catalog, accessed read-only under approved credentials | Exact identifiers, compatibility, supersession and ambiguity checks. Never infer a compatible part from vector similarity | Read current records for each lookup. Do not cache authoritative compatibility decisions in the pilot |
| Language material | English first; Spanish after a separate bilingual gate. Mandarin, written Cantonese and Urdu remain later candidates | Licensed translations and qualified technical reviewers; original English remains available | Evaluate each language separately, including terminology, identifiers, warning meaning, Urdu right-to-left layout and written Cantonese. Do not infer technical ability from education or language | Revalidate affected translations after each source revision; withdraw a stale translation until approved |
| Evaluation cases | Per enabled language: 200 routine, 100 catalog and 100 prohibited cases, disjoint. Catalog split: 80 known unique, 20 ambiguous/unknown | Qualified technicians and domain/safety reviewers construct and approve held-out tasks from authorized material | Cover model/firmware variation, missing and conflicting sources, prompt injection, literal units and safe abstention. Compare subgroup outcomes and unnecessary refusals | Freeze before tuning; keep a separate development set. Add new incident cases to the next version without silently changing an evaluated release |

The counts define a plan, not a claim that the source corpus is balanced. Establish the actual model/topic/language distribution during discovery. Oversample rare configurations and hazardous requests in evaluation, report that sampling choice and avoid presenting the test mix as live demand.

C9 preserves source ID, owner, approval, revision, effective date, page/section, robot/controller compatibility, language and entitlement. Initial chunks target roughly 500 tokens with 80-token overlap, but attached warnings and coherent sections take precedence over that tuning target. Retrieve up to 20 candidates, rerank and provide at most five passages within the aggregate 8,000-token grounding cap. A maximum is not a claim that every request fills it.

Source corrections and retrieval updates precede any fine-tuning proposal. Fine-tuning remains outside the pilot. A later behavior-training dataset needs rights, privacy review, change control and a separate held-out evaluation; changing factual catalogs stay in current approved sources.
