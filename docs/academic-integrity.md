## Finding

Submission eligibility is not established. The [Honor Code](https://www.udacity.com/en-US/legal/honor-code), updated 5 February 2026, permits supplementary AI help and prohibits AI producing assessed work in whole or substantial part. It requires submitted work to reflect the student's personal effort and understanding. Disclosure applies where course guidelines permit use; disclosure alone does not authorize otherwise prohibited assistance. Its stated limit is that AI use "must not replace the student's own independent thinking, analysis, or execution."

The known workflow includes AI creation of substantive product reasoning, architecture choices, calculations and slide content. Student authorship of those judgments has not been demonstrated in this workflow. This provenance does not support an agent certifying an own-work declaration. It is not a Udacity adjudication about the learner.
