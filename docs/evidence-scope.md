# Evidence boundaries

The deliverable is a product proposal and slide deck. It is not an implemented industrial advisor.

Version 1.1.0 also carries an unresolved academic-eligibility item. Substantive reasoning, architecture, content and code were AI-generated. Accurate attribution and artifact verification do not establish the student's own assessed work. Current policy and classroom observations are recorded in the academic-integrity and revision-response documents; no classroom submission or successful-submission confirmation is claimed.

The user supplied the hypothetical business scenario and assessment requirements. Knowledgebase counts, device access and supported languages describe that scenario. They are not findings from company records. Device and education percentages overlap and must not be added into distributions.

Vendor documentation supports the stated interfaces, retrieval patterns, privacy qualifications and adjacent market examples. A documented model does not establish account access, regional availability, industrial suitability, throughput or multilingual quality. Vendor descriptions do not establish comparative superiority.

All product KPI thresholds, staffing, delivery dates relative to pilot start, adoption shares, time savings and budgets are proposals. No product benchmark or customer outcome was measured. The economic worksheet estimates redeployable capacity, not cash savings, revenue or avoided downtime. The conservative case is negative; the base case has a long simple implementation payback.

The proposal requires independent technical reviewers, controlled source material and qualified safety personnel before supervised use. Curated refusal cases can expose failures but cannot certify safety. Citation and catalog checks cannot prove an explanation is safe. Existing site procedures remain authoritative.

Microsoft's current model documentation contains conflicting descriptions of sampling-parameter support. The proposal therefore omits a temperature override. Endpoint compatibility, contract terms, retention, data location and the tested model version must be confirmed before customer content is used.

Repository checks concern artifact integrity, arithmetic and presentation structure. A sandboxed static scan and a bounded semantic advisory review have narrower recorded scopes. Neither gives deployment approval or predicts an Udacity grade. All generated supporting material is public-safe; raw course text, account paths, credentials and private prior project documents are excluded.
